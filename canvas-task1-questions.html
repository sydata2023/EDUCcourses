Start on your own
Take a minute on your own: based on science classes you've taken, what makes a question “scientific” rather than just curious? Jot a quick note for yourself — you won't turn this in, just bring it with you into your group.
Name your group
Choose a name that reflects who you are and how you want to approach this investigation. This will be your group's discussion thread for the whole module.
Write your questions
Working together, write at least three scientific questions your group could investigate about bread mold growth. Post them under your group's thread.
Before you post:
Make sure each question is something you could actually test — not just something you're curious about.
Post under your own group's thread, not a new one.
