Module 1 · Weeks 1–2

Where We're Starting: Beliefs About Learning & Teaching →

Big idea: Surface beliefs about learning, teaching, and science before any framework — establish the baseline the whole semester reflects back on.

Key topics: Naive theories of learning & teaching · peer teaching without a framework · perspective-taking
Module 2 · Weeks 3–4

Doing Science, Planning Science →

Big idea: Experience real inquiry firsthand, then learn the standards and tools for planning it intentionally.

Key topics: Levels of inquiry · Nature of Science · NGSS three-dimensional structure · 5E lesson-design model
Module 3 · Weeks 5–6

Asking Good Questions & Listening to Student Thinking →

Big idea: Learn to surface what students already think and build on it, instead of just checking for right answers.

Key topics: Eliciting student thinking · using data to adjust instruction · productive talk moves
Module 4 · Weeks 7–8

Making Thinking Visible & Getting Field-Ready →

Big idea: Use models to see thinking change, and get concretely ready for residency.

Key topics: Field-readiness logistics · using models formatively · equity check-in on curriculum
Module 5 · Weeks 9–10

Out in the Classroom: Residency →

Big idea: Enact ambitious-teaching practices in a live classroom; use collective sense-making to move activity into learning.

Key topics: Supporting ongoing changes in thinking · analyzing real teaching · interdisciplinary connections
Module 6 · Week 11

Backing It Up: Evidence, Reasoning & Reflecting on Practice →

Big idea: Debrief residency evidence and build claim-evidence-reasoning explanations.

Key topics: Argumentation & evidence-based reasoning · connecting field evidence to theory
Module 7 · Weeks 12–13

Teaching for Every Student: 3D + Equity Lesson Plan Capstone →

Big idea: Build one full NGSS-aligned lesson, informed by residency and equity tools, and teach it to peers.

Key topics: Planning from a performance expectation & DCI · designing for practice & crosscutting concepts · peer review · capstone peer teaching
Module 8 · Weeks 14–15

Growing as a Professional: Revisiting Where We Started →

Big idea: Use colleagues to keep improving, and see how far your own teaching and thinking have come.

Key topics: Comparing revised practice to baseline · lesson study · course synthesis
