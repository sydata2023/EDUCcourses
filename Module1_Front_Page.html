Key Activities

Bread Mold Growth Investigation

Students design and run their own open-ended investigation on Day 1, before any teaching framework is introduced.

Why: Lets candidates feel the real messiness of inquiry — picking a variable, troubleshooting failure — before they're asked to plan investigations for K–5 students.

Baseline Co-Teaching

Pairs teach a 10-minute lesson to peers, video recorded, with no framework or coaching beforehand.

Why: Captures an honest "before" snapshot of instinct-driven teaching. This becomes the anchor artifact the whole semester circles back to.

Concept Map + Video Reflection

Synthesizes the personal-definition, Fish is Fish, and Good Science Lesson activities into one map and a short reflection video.

Why: Consolidates scattered belief-elicitation work into a single reference point candidates and the instructor can return to later for comparison.

Looking ahead: The Baseline Co-Teaching video and Concept Map you create in Module 1 will resurface repeatedly — most directly in Module 8, when you re-teach this same lesson and compare it side by side with where you started.
