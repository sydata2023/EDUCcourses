Introduce yourself: your path to teaching, and where science fits in it

Discussion Forum 1 — Becoming a Teacher, Becoming a Science Teacher

📝 Text, audio, or video (2–3 min) 👥 Reply to 2 classmates ✅ Credit / no-credit
Take a few minutes to introduce yourself to the class. You can write your response, or if you'd rather talk it through, record a short audio or video clip instead — either format is completely fine.
1

Tell us about you

Your name, and something you want us to know about you — where you're from, what you did before this program, whatever feels relevant.

2

When did you decide to become a teacher?

Was there a moment, a person, a class you sat in, a realization? Take us there.

There's no "right" story — a lifelong certainty and a decision made three weeks ago both count.

3

Do you see yourself as a science teacher?

Why or why not? Be honest — this isn't a test of enthusiasm. If science feels like a subject you're excited to teach, say why. If it's the subject you're most nervous about, say that too, and say what's behind the nervousness.

Then: reply to at least two classmates

Connect their story to yours — where do your paths overlap, and where do they diverge? If someone named a worry about teaching science, respond as a future colleague, not a grader.
