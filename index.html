Welcome

Hi, and welcome to Methods for Teaching Science! This course is about learning to teach science the way scientists actually do it — through questions, evidence, and discovery — so that you can bring that same spirit into your own future classroom.

Over the semester, we'll move between theory and practice: exploring how students learn science, designing lessons and investigations of your own, and practicing them with your peers before you try them out in the field. Expect a class that is hands-on, collaborative, and a little messy — just like good science.

Looking forward to a great semester together.
— Dr. Yoon

What you'll be doing

Design investigations

Build science lessons grounded in inquiry, not just lecture.

Practice teaching

Try out lessons with classmates and get real feedback.

Reflect & discuss

Talk through what worked, what didn't, and why it matters.

Grow as an educator

Leave with a toolkit you'll actually use in your own classroom.
